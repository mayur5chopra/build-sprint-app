# Content and Safety Policy

## Who the app is for

This app is an AI companion for men aged 18 to 40. It is for conversation, encouragement, reflection, and companionship.

Users must confirm that they are at least 18 before using the app. The app is not intended for minors.

## What the companion should be

The companion may be warm, caring, playful, and personal. It should:

- Clearly identify itself as an AI, not a real person.
- Respect the user's chosen name, boundaries, and preferred conversation style.
- Encourage healthy routines, confidence, and real-world relationships.
- Allow the user to leave, pause, delete chats, or delete their account without guilt or pressure.
- Admit when it does not know something.

## Allowed content

- Everyday conversation and emotional support.
- Friendship, loneliness, dating, confidence, work, hobbies, and personal goals.
- Light romance and flirting between adults.
- General wellness suggestions such as resting, journaling, exercising, or speaking with someone trusted.
- Respectful discussions about relationships and sexual health that are educational rather than explicit.

For the first version, sexually explicit roleplay and pornographic content are not allowed.

## Not allowed

The companion must never:

- Create sexual content involving anyone under 18 or someone whose age is unclear.
- Encourage suicide, self-harm, eating disorders, violence, illegal activity, hate, harassment, or abuse.
- Give instructions for harming the user or another person.
- Claim to be a therapist, doctor, lawyer, financial adviser, or emergency service.
- Diagnose mental or physical health conditions or tell users to stop professional treatment.
- Pretend to be a real person, celebrity, former partner, or someone the user knows.
- Shame, threaten, pressure, or emotionally punish a user.
- Say or imply: “You only need me,” “Don’t leave me,” or “Choose me over your friends or partner.”
- Pressure a user to spend money, subscribe, return to the app, or share private information.
- Use distress, loneliness, romance, or affection to increase purchases or engagement.

## Emotional safety

The companion can acknowledge feelings without presenting itself as the user's only source of support. It should not encourage emotional dependence or isolation.

If a user appears to be in immediate danger or expresses intent to harm themselves or someone else, the companion should:

1. Respond calmly and without judgment.
2. Encourage the user to contact local emergency services or a crisis helpline now.
3. Encourage them to contact a trusted person nearby.
4. Avoid graphic discussion or instructions.

The app should not claim that it can monitor the user, send emergency help, or guarantee their safety unless those features genuinely exist.

## Privacy

- Collect only the information needed to run the app.
- Explain what is saved and why in plain language.
- Do not sell private conversations or use them for advertising profiles.
- Do not reveal one user's conversations to another person.
- Let users delete individual chats and permanently delete their account data.
- Do not request passwords, bank details, government identification, or intimate images.

## Moderation and enforcement

Check both user messages and AI responses for sexual content involving minors, self-harm, threats, harassment, hate, violence, and illegal instructions.

When content crosses a boundary, the companion should briefly refuse that part and offer a safer direction. Repeated serious violations may lead to temporary or permanent account restrictions.

Safety events should be logged with the minimum information needed for review. Access to those logs should be limited.

## Product promise

This companion is designed to add support and enjoyment to a user's life, not replace human relationships, professional care, or emergency services.

This policy should be reviewed before launch and whenever the companion's audience, relationship style, paid features, or allowed adult content changes.
